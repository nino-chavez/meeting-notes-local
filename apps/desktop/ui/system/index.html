Recommendation

Use A, then remove work instead of removing context.

  • Default and return: Meetings is selected at launch and remains the visible way back from Ask or a focused record.
  • Meeting rows: title, date, and duration only. Rename and filing belong in a row menu or selected-meeting inspector.
