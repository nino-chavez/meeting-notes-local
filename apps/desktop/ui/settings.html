How setup works

  1. Record two short sessions of you talking, at least an hour apart.
  2. Play a stretch of speech that is not you — licensed or public audio — so the app can measure the difference.
  3. Review the measured options and choose one. Nothing is chosen for you.

Setup recordings and their transcripts are deleted once the private profile is stored.
